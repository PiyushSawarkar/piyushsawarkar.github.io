Sep 2026

Talk at Mixture of Experts, hosted by Activate & BharatGen

Friday, 11 September 2026 · SINE Co-Working Space, Rahul Bajaj Technology Innovation Centre (RBTIC), IIT Bombay Campus, Powai, Mumbai, India

Invited to speak alongside Prof. Ganesh Ramakrishnan at Mixture of Experts, Activate's series bringing together researchers, research engineers, and technical builders working at the frontiers of AI. We spoke on "From Tokens to Reasoning to Intelligent Agentic AI Systems: Building a Sovereign Foundational AI Stack." The talk focused on what it actually takes to build a foundation model from scratch, going beyond pretraining alone.

Drawing on experience building sovereign foundation models at BharatGen, I walked through the full stack: large-scale pretraining, post-training and reinforcement learning, evaluation and reasoning, agentic harnesses, and ultimately population-scale deployment of capable AI systems.

The central message was that building a foundation model is not a single training run, but an end-to-end engineering and research journey — from learning from massive corpora, to developing reasoning and decision-making capabilities through post-training and RL, to building the infrastructure and harnesses required to turn models into reliable intelligent agents at scale.

Sep 2026

Talk at the Columbia–IIT Bombay AI for Manufacturing (CI-AIM) Industry Conclave 2026

Friday, 4 September 2026 · IIT Bombay Research Park, ASPIRE, Mumbai, India

Spoke on "Building a Sovereign Industrial Foundation Model Stack in Consortium Mode", a proposal for how industries could jointly train an Industrial Foundation Model without ever sharing proprietary data. Drawing on experience building foundation models end-to-end at BharatGen, I laid out a federated approach where sovereign industrial nodes keep their data, IP and operating environments to themselves, while still contributing to a shared model.

The core idea — "Bring Your Own Expertise" — combines off-policy learning from diverse industrial demonstrations with local on-policy reinforcement learning, benchmarked across realistic industrial environments to push cross-domain generalisation. The result: an Industrial Foundation Model more capable than any single company could build alone, sitting at the intersection of sovereign AI, federated learning and manufacturing intelligence.

Visiting Lecture on Reinforcement Learning, IIT Bombay2 × 3hr masterclasses

Friday, 14 August 2026 & Sunday, 16 August 2026 · CSE Department (CC 101) and the Rahul Bajaj Technology Innovation Centre, BharatGen, IIT Bombay

Invited by Prof. Aditya Desai to guest lecture for CS6013: Efficient AI at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Bombay. Delivered two 3+ hour masterclasses on "From Imitation to Reasoning to Agentic — Scaling Large Language Models through Reinforcement Learning", tracing the evolution of RL-based post-training in modern LLMs.

Topics spanned on-policy and off-policy learning, algorithms from TRPO, PPO and GRPO through to on-policy self-distillation and multi-teacher distillation, reward and environment design, curriculum learning, distributed RL infrastructure, verifiable versus unverifiable domains, provenance mapping and test-time compute optimisation — grounded throughout in the practical challenges of scaling RL for large foundation models at BharatGen.

Jun 2026

Talk at Advances in Proteomics Technologies Conference 20261000+ attendees

Friday, 29 June 2026 · Lecture Hall Complex, IIT Bombay, Mumbai, India

Delivered "Building India's Sovereign AI Stack: From Tokens to Foundational-Scale LLMs to Intelligent Agents" to an audience of 1,000+, including international delegates, IIT Bombay students and visiting high-school scholars. The talk walked through BharatGen's blueprint for India's sovereign AI mission, centred on Param2 — a 17-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model trained on 22 trillion tokens across 22 Indian languages.

I covered architectural choices behind the model — low-fertility tokenization for Indic scripts, shared-and-routed MoE routing, and GRPO-based alignment for agentic reasoning — and, for the life-sciences audience specifically, showed how the same foundation model stack is fine-tuned for genomic understanding and multi-omics integration, reasoning across protein-protein interactions, epigenomics and variant interpretation.
