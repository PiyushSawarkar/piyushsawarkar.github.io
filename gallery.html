Talk at Mixture of Experts, hosted by Activate & BharatGen

Friday, 11 September 2026 SINE Co-Working Space, RBTIC, IIT Bombay, Powai, Mumbai, India

Invited to speak alongside Prof. Ganesh Ramakrishnan at Mixture of Experts, Activate’s series bringing together researchers, research engineers, and technical builders working at the frontiers of AI. We spoke on “From Tokens to Reasoning to Intelligent Agentic AI Systems: Building a Sovereign Foundational AI Stack,” walking through the full stack — large-scale pretraining, post-training and reinforcement learning, evaluation and reasoning, agentic harnesses, and population-scale deployment. The central message: building a foundation model is not a single training run, but an end-to-end engineering and research journey.

Talk at the Columbia–IIT Bombay AI for Manufacturing (CI-AIM) Industry Conclave 2026

Friday, 4 September 2026 IIT Bombay Research Park, ASPIRE, Mumbai, India

Spoke on “Building a Sovereign Industrial Foundation Model Stack in Consortium Mode” — a proposal for how industries could jointly train an Industrial Foundation Model without ever sharing proprietary data. Drawing on experience building foundation models end-to-end at BharatGen, I laid out a federated approach where sovereign industrial nodes keep their data, IP and operating environments to themselves, while still contributing to a shared model. The core idea — “Bring Your Own Expertise” — combines off-policy learning from diverse industrial demonstrations with local on-policy reinforcement learning, benchmarked across realistic industrial environments to push cross-domain generalisation.

Panel Discussion at IndiaAI Mission’s AIKosh Workshop 2026

Thursday, 20 August 2026 India Habitat Centre, New Delhi, India

Panel speaker on “What the Developer Ecosystem Needs”, moderated by Dr. Santosh Misra (PwC), alongside leaders from government, industry, startups and the broader AI ecosystem. The conversation covered small and edge models, data requirements for AI builders, responsible data ecosystems and talent development for India. Representing BharatGen, I made the case for treating data as a first-class component of AI development — provenance, metadata, quality, licensing and principled ingestion — and for giving developers straightforward, AI-ready access to datasets through AIKosh.

Visiting Lecture on Reinforcement Learning, IIT Bombay

Friday, 14 & Sunday, 16 August 2026 CSE Department (CC 101) & RBTIC, BharatGen, IIT Bombay

Invited by Prof. Aditya Desai to guest lecture for CS6013: Efficient AI at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Bombay. Delivered two 3+ hour masterclasses on “From Imitation to Reasoning to Agentic — Scaling Large Language Models through Reinforcement Learning,” going beyond the standard treatment of RL algorithms to look at how RL is actually used to post-train modern LLMs — from reasoning and test-time compute to agentic behaviour. Topics spanned off-policy vs. on-policy learning, the algorithm zoo from TRPO to PPO to GRPO, on-policy self-distillation, multi-teacher distillation, RL environments and reward design, distributed RL infrastructure, verifiable vs. unverifiable domains, provenance mapping, curriculum design, and test-time compute optimisation — grounded throughout in the practical challenges of scaling RL for large foundation models at BharatGen.

With Prof. Aditya Desai

Talk at Advances in Proteomics Technologies Conference 2026

Friday, 29 June 2026 Lecture Hall Complex, IIT Bombay, Mumbai, India

Delivered “Building India’s Sovereign AI Stack: From Tokens to Foundational-Scale LLMs to Intelligent Agents” to an audience of 1,000+, including international delegates, IIT Bombay students and visiting high-school scholars. The talk walked through BharatGen’s blueprint for India’s sovereign AI mission, centred on Param2 — a 17-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model trained on 22 trillion tokens across 22 Indian languages — and, for the life-sciences audience specifically, how the same foundation model stack is fine-tuned for genomic understanding and multi-omics integration.

With Prof. Ganesh Ramakrishnan
